# WI-20261121-ATS-004 local pilot frontend

The friends pilot adds individual downloads on the public catalog for signed-in participants. Participants can revisit their download history, download a track again and look up their own license code. Visitors retain public listening. Signup, sales and bulk downloads remain outside the pilot routes.

The local frontend passed 142 focused tests across 10 files, TypeScript checks, ESLint and a pilot-mode production build. Rendered tests covered a public track download and switching between two sample participants. Their data came from a local test adapter. Live accounts and real-device verification belong to later stages. The next gate is independent review.
